# Provenance and limits

This package generalises the manifest, parameter, packaging, and artifact-freshness patterns
used by [VOLLEY](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY),
[BOLLEY](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/BOLLEY), and
[engineering-evidence-toolkit](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/engineering-evidence-toolkit).

A matching hash proves byte-level reproducibility of a named artifact. It does not establish
geometric correctness, manufacturability, or physical validation.
